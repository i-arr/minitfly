Welcomer posts a styled welcome card whenever someone joins. You choose the channel and write the message.

## Setup from the panel

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the panel">
    `{prefix}setup` → **Page 5** → click **Welcomer**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set the channel">
    Click **Add**, enter the channel where welcomes should appear, **Submit**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Write the message">
    Open the message form and type your welcome text. Leave it as-is to keep the default:
    `Welcome to {guild.name}, {user.mention}!`
  </Step>
  <Step title="Test">
    Invite an alt (or just trust the preview on the panel) — the card posts with your Theme accent color.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Message variables

| Write | Becomes |
|---|---|
| `{user.mention}` | @the new member |
| `{user.name}` | their username |
| `{guild.name}` | your server name |

<Info>
  The Welcomer card is what members see first — pair it with **Auto Role** (Page 1) so joining feels instant, and with **Logs Server** so staff notice too.
</Info>

<Warning>
  Point Welcomer at a channel where every member can **View Channel**, otherwise the greeting posts into invisibility.
</Warning>
